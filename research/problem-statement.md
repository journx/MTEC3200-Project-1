# Problem Statement

When I ____, I have to ____ which is frustrating because ____. I think this happens because ____. What I wish happened instead is ____.

## Self-Check

- Would a stranger understand this without me explaining it?
- Does it name a specific person, not a demographic?
- Does it avoid naming a solution?

  When I don't fall asleep sooner in the night, I have to wake up early feeling unrefreshed and groggy, which is frustrating because if I don't get out of bed, I'm prone to staying in bed all day because of my mental health. I think this happens because I take a long time to fall asleep, and my brain is way more active at night. What I wish happened instead was me falling asleep as soon as I lie my head down and then I'd be more refreshed in the morning so I wouldn't be taking extra time to sleep and then rushing to class or work.
